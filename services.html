Reflections

Sebuah refleksi singkat tentang perjalanan belajar, proses berkembang secara pribadi, serta pengalaman berharga yang membentuk cara pandang saya selama menempuh kehidupan perkuliahan.

Transition to University Life

Adapting from high school to university was a major step that required independence, self-discipline, and adjusting to a new academic environment.

Academic Challenges & Deadlines

Managing multiple deadlines taught me time management, responsibility, and how to stay productive under pressure.

HIMTI & Leadership Growth

Joining HIMTI provided valuable experience in leadership, teamwork, and organizing student activities that strengthened my communication skills.

Collaboration & Communication

Working in teams during projects helped me understand the importance of clear communication and collective problem-solving.

Learning from Feedback

Receiving feedback from lecturers and peers taught me to improve continuously and view mistakes as part of the growth process.

Shaping Future Mindset

All these experiences contributed to building a resilient mindset, preparing me for professional challenges beyond university.
